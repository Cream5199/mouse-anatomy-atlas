This supplement contains 56 independently selectable objects:
12 digestive/airway/urinary conduit structures, 18 major arterial/venous
regional trees, and 26 major skeletal muscle groups. The unchanged original
27 objects are separately distributed under /models/digimouse-voxel/.

Method:
Curved surfaces and tubes are authored around the original Digimouse organ
and skeletal landmarks. Muscle lofts include narrowing tendon regions,
longitudinal relief, and linear RGB vertex colors. Intestinal loops have
irregular orientation and depth, with a distinct murine caecum. Vessel trees
use tapering regional branches and selected mouse-specific trunk connections.
All meshes share the original raw millimetre coordinate system. The viewer
uses one common centering, scale and X-axis rotation for every source.
A/B are coordinate-side groups, not verified anatomical laterality.

These are teaching approximations, NOT measured specimen segmentations.
Curves, branching, calibres, loop number and muscle surfaces are authored.
Not all microvessels, nerves, muscle attachments, organ lobes or histological
layers are supplied. Connected parts may meet as intersecting closed surfaces;
there is no manufacturing, surgical, flow-simulation or lumen continuity claim.
Reference drawings are consulted for factual anatomy, not copied as images.
