Adaptation by Mouse Atlas:
Read the official Analyze uint8 label volume in its original storage order
(380 x 992 x 208, 0.1 mm isotropic voxels), and extracted each non-background
label with step-1 Lewiner marching cubes at the label boundary. Preserved all
21 source classes, relative positions and source coordinates. No geometry
smoothing, decimation, subdivision, invented organs or new segmentation.
Added Chinese labels and illustrative colors. Converted to indexed GLB.
Rounded NORMAL attributes to five decimal places for compression; POSITION
and triangle-index bytes are unchanged by this optimization. Maximum normal
direction change is 0.000484 degrees. Gzip compression is lossless.

Delivery:
core.glb.gz contains 26 internal objects from 20 source labels (2,819,068 triangles).
skin.glb.gz.part1 and skin.glb.gz.part2 contain consecutive bytes of one
gzip stream. Concatenate part1 followed by part2, then decompress once to
obtain the optional body-region GLB (3,889,924 triangles).
The viewer loads the body region only when requested, displays it transparent
in the whole-body view, and opaque during isolation. All regions receive the
same display centering, uniform scaling and 180-degree X rotation. Exploded
positions are reversible display transforms, not anatomical coordinates.

Scope:
21 macro-level labels are not 21 distinct organ types. Six paired labels have
been split into two complete connected components each: eyes, kidneys, adrenal
glands, testes, lachrymal glands and masseter muscles. All original faces,
positions and rounded normals were preserved. There are 27 selectable objects
including the original body region. A/B indicate source-coordinate components,
not verified anatomical laterality. The entire skeleton is one group. Fine per-bone, per-muscle,
vascular and peripheral-nerve segmentation is not provided. Source header
information does not justify assigning anatomical left/right to unnamed pairs.
